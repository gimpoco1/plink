Score tracker for real game nights

Keep scores, sessions, players, and stats in one place.

Plink is a scorekeeping app for board games, card games, party games, team matches, and recurring casual sessions. Use it to start a scoreboard quickly, track score changes, resume unfinished games, and keep useful history after the winner is decided.

Live scoreboards

Track individual players or teams with custom point steps, target scores, timers, dice, and clear winner rules.

Game history

Save past sessions so results, score changes, player progress, and repeated setups are easy to review later.

Shared games

Invite other players to the same scoreboard so several accounts can update the game from their own devices.
